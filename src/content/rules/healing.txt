Healing

To heal in combat takes a move action. I could shoot a gun and heal in the same turn. Success is automatic. Providing first aid to a Hero below 0 HP stops their Death Clock as a move action. Out of combat, you heal the max, but it takes one minute. Through natural healing, a Hero fully heals after sleep and regains all energy points.

Trauma patches. These are medical devices that you use when wounded. It takes an attack action, and you can use as many as you want at once. It costs 2 Gold per Trauma Patch and it heals 2d4 per patch.

Hero Death

Deathwatch: the Death Clock is ticking.

A Hero with "0" HP can only take a move action. When a Hero drops below "0" HP, they are dying.

At the end of each turn, the Hero makes an Endurance TN11 roll. Each time the Hero fails the roll, the Death Clock increases by 1. The Death Clock stops when the Hero makes the roll or a teammate provides first aid.

Now roll a d6. If the roll is less than the Death Clock, the Hero dies; otherwise, the Hero stabilizes.

Heal - this allows the Hero to take the max heal. This takes one minute. If the effect is granted by another Hero, that hero must spend one minute of time as well.
