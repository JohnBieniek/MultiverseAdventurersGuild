Hero Death

Deathwatch: the clock is ticking.

A Hero with "0" HP can only take a move action. When a Hero drops below "0" HP, they are dying.

At the end of each turn, the Hero makes an Endurance TN11 roll. Each time the Hero fails the roll, the clock increases by 1. The clock stops when the Hero makes the roll or a teammate provides first aid.

Now roll a d6. If the roll is less than the clock, the Hero dies; otherwise, the Hero stabilizes.
